Build Auron Business OS — Event Company Management & Business Intelligence Platform

Build a production-ready full-stack web application called Auron Business OS for Auron Event Productions, an event production and management company in Kerala, India.

This is an internal management platform for the CEO/management team. The purpose is to track event-level profitability, client acquisition, repeat business, sales pipeline, cash flow, vendors, assets, and company growth — ultimately helping management work toward a ₹90 crore company valuation goal.

Do NOT build this as a simple static dashboard or demo. Build a functional application with a real database, authentication, CRUD operations, calculations, filters, charts, and persistent data.

36. IMPORTANT ACCOUNTING LOGIC

Clearly separate:

Revenue

Money earned from events.

Direct Costs

Costs directly attributable to an event.

Gross Profit

Revenue - Direct Costs.

Operating Expenses

Company-level expenses not directly attributable to one event.

EBITDA

Gross Profit - Operating Expenses before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization.

Do not mix these calculations.

GST should be tracked separately from operating revenue/profit where appropriate.

Do not treat GST collected as company revenue.
